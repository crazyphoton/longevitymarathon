What his role is

Dr. Reddy reviews the training plan, provides evidence-based commentary as the project runs, and helps keep the science side of this site honest. He is a collaborator and reviewer — not a treating clinician for readers, and nothing published under his name here should be read as individual medical advice. See the medical disclaimer for the full boundary.

How his commentary will be attributed

Every piece of commentary published under his name will be clearly attributed, dated, and marked as one of three things: a summary of existing evidence, his clinical or expert judgment, or a project-specific suggestion. Those are different kinds of claims, and this site doesn't flatten them into one voice. Where he and the author disagree, the disagreement is preserved and shown, not resolved quietly in the edit.

Where you'll see him first

His first formal review is of the current training plan — see the pending "Doctor review" section on the Plan page and the disagreement-sequence preview on the homepage, both currently missing his voice on purpose.
