Weekly load — duration first

Plan v3 is duration-led: the marathon is a time-on-feet problem, so minutes and long-run share are the primary exposure readouts and distance is context. Session-RPE load (effort × minutes, summed for the week) is the internal-load backbone — it survives heat, hills, and a flaky optical sensor. Amber days count the control loop's cautions; symptom detail stays private.
