Plan v2 — current

17 weeks across four phases, effective 10 Aug 2026. Replaces v1 after a self-initiated revision three days before training began.

Current Effective from 10 Aug 2026· Created by: author· Reviewed by: Dr. Reddy (pending)

Summary: a four-phase build (Foundation, Build, Peak, Taper) with a cutback week every fourth week, two strength sessions weekly, and a peak long run of 32 km in Week 13 — six weeks later than v1 scheduled the same distance.

Change reason: v1's mileage ramp broke the ~10%-per-week guideline badly enough in the first five weeks that the author revised it unprompted, before the formal doctor review began. Full reasoning in the journal entry.
