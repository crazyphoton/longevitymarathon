Plan v1 — original (superseded)

The author's first lay draft, preserved exactly as written. It was never trained on — it was replaced three days before Week 1.

Superseded Drafted 31 Jul 2026· Replaced 9 Aug 2026· Created by: author

Summary: a straight-line mileage ramp from 25 km to 55 km over five weeks, held flat afterward, with one strength session a week and no cutback weeks. Only drafted in detail through Week 9 — the later weeks were never fully written before the revision.

Why it was replaced

The jump from Week 1 to Week 5 is roughly a 120% mileage increase in five weeks, with the long run reaching 32 km — the plan's eventual peak distance — by Week 6, and no cutback week anywhere in the structure. Measured against the commonly cited 10%-per-week guideline, that ramp is aggressive enough that the author revised it unprompted; see v2 and the journal entry explaining the change.
