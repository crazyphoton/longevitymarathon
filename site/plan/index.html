Change history

  • 9 Aug 2026
    v1 → v2: mileage ramp smoothed, cutback weeks added

    What changed: weekly mileage progression rebuilt against the ~10%-per-week guideline; cutback weeks added at Weeks 4, 8, and 12; peak long run moved from Week 6 to Week 13; strength sessions doubled from one to two per week; the two hardest sessions of each week separated by at least one easy day.

    Peak weekly mileage: 55 km, reached by Week 5 → 47 km, reached by Week 11

    Why: re-reading v1 next to standard progression guidance made the jump from 25 km to 55 km over five weeks look reckless before a single session had been run.

    Evidence/experience informing the change: the commonly cited 10%-per-week mileage guideline and a gut check against past training history — not Dr. Reddy's review, which was still pending.

    Dr. Reddy's view: not yet recorded — his review of v2 is in progress.

    Affected weeks: all 17.

    Outcome: awaiting — will be updated as the plan plays out. See the journal entry for the full reasoning.

Plan states used on this site

Each session can be: planned, completed as planned, completed with modification, moved, skipped, stopped, or awaiting outcome. Minor copy fixes don't create a new plan version; anything that changes training does.
