Source & method

Garmin, nightly, read as a rolling weekly average.

Measurement caveats

Night-to-night RHR is noisy: alcohol, poor sleep, illness, heat, and travel all move it independently of fitness. Single-night readings are not reported as meaningful on their own; the site prefers a rolling average and flags unusual single nights rather than treating them as trend.
