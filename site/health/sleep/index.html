Sleep

Why it may matter here

Sleep is both a recovery input to training and a confound for nearly every other marker on this dashboard — it needs to be visible on its own, not just implied.

Measurement caveats

Consumer sleep staging is a lower-confidence estimate than a clinical sleep study. Duration is treated as more reliable than the light/deep/REM breakdown.
