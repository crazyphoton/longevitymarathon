Source & method

Home reading, validated monitor, seated and rested per standard guidance; posture and repeat readings noted.

Measurement caveats

Posture, recent caffeine, recent exercise, and cuff size all move a single reading. Multiple seated readings are preferred over one; readings are reported with those conditions attached.
