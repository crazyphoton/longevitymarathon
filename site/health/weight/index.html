Weight

Body mass, tracked on a consistent scale under consistent conditions (same time of day, similar clothing).

Why it may matter here

The simplest, noisiest body-composition signal, useful mainly in combination with body fat and muscle mass rather than alone — weight changes don't say whether what changed was fat, muscle, or water.
