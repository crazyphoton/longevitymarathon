VO₂ max

The maximum rate at which the body can take up and use oxygen during hard exercise, usually expressed in millilitres of oxygen per kilogram of body weight per minute (mL/kg/min).

Why it may matter here

It's one of the better single predictors of cardiorespiratory fitness and is associated with long-term health outcomes in population research — and it's the marker most directly targeted by an endurance training block.

Read the measurement methodology

Source & method

Garmin device estimate (Firstbeat algorithm) from run data; a laboratory gas-exchange test if one is scheduled.

Measurement caveats

Garmin's estimate is a model, not a direct measurement — it can drift with heat, fatigue, or a change in running economy that has nothing to do with actual aerobic capacity. It is tracked as a trend, not a lab-grade number, and never merged with a lab result if one is later obtained.
